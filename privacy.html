Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

MultiPost Studio is a personal content management tool designed to help users manage and publish content on supported social media platforms.

2. Information and Account Access

When users connect a social media account, the application may receive authorization tokens and account information required to provide the requested features.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide account connection, content management, and publishing features requested by the user.

4. Data Storage and Security

Authentication tokens are stored locally on the user's device where supported. They are used to communicate with the relevant social media platforms. Reasonable measures are taken to protect locally stored data.

5. Sharing of Information

MultiPost Studio does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to a connected platform when the user requests an action such as publishing content.

6. Third-Party Services

Connected platforms have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review those policies before connecting their accounts.

7. Data Deletion

Users can disconnect their accounts through the application where supported and may delete locally stored application data from their device.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact us at: multipost.official90@gmail.com
