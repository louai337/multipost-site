MultiPost Studio

MultiPost Studio is a personal content management tool designed to help manage and publish videos across supported social media platforms.

Privacy Policy

MultiPost Studio uses account authorization only to provide the publishing features requested by its user. Authentication tokens are stored securely on the user's own device and are not sold or shared with advertisers.

The application does not intentionally collect personal information beyond what is necessary to provide its features.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the application owner.
